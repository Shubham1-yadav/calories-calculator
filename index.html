About CalorieCalc

CalorieCalc is a simple educational calorie calculator that estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and daily calorie needs using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and activity level.

These calculations are estimates for educational purposes and should not be considered medical advice.
